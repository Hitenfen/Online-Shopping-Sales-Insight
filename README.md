# Online-Shopping-Sales-Insight
The Online Shopping Annual Report Dashboard is an interactive Excel dashboard developed to analyze and visualize annual sales performance for an online retail business. The dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of sales trends, customer demographics, product categories, sales channels, order status, and regional performance.

Tech Stack: Microsoft Excel | Data Analysis | Dashboard Design | Business Intelligence

📈 Key Insights
Women contribute 64% of total sales, while men account for 36%.
Amazon and Myntra are among the highest-performing sales channels.
Maharashtra and Karnataka generate the highest sales revenue.
Over 90% of orders are successfully delivered.
Peak sales occur during the first quarter of the year.

📊 Dashboard KPIs

Sales by Gender: Customer segmentation by age and gender
Age-wise Customer Distribution: Gender-wise sales comparison
Monthly Orders vs Sales: Monthly sales trend analysis
Top 5 Sales Performing States: Highest revenue-generating states
Order Status Distribution: Delivery, cancellation, return, and refund statistics
